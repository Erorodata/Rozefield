Rozefield Backup - save your OverField account before the official servers close (8 Feb 2027)

1. Put this folder anywhere (it needs nothing installed).
2. Start "Rozefield Backup.exe" and press Open game (or Use running game if OverField is already open).
3. Log in with your official account as usual. The window collects your account by itself.
4. When it says Everything collected, press Save backup (or just close the game).

Your backup is saved in the "backups" folder next to the exe:  backups\<name>_<date>.rzsave
Keep that file. After the shutdown, import it on any Rozefield server: in the Rozefield login window choose
Import official backup.

Nothing in your official account is changed. The backup never keeps your login token or device details.
